1Requirements to get your P1

Age

Under 25 → Red P

Drivers under 25 years old receive a P1 (Red P) licence as their first provisional stage. You must be at least 17 years old.

Learner tenure

12 months + 100 hours

You must have held your Learner licence for 12 months and finished your 100 logbook hours (including 10 hours at night) before applying.

Test

Practical driving test

You must pass the practical driving test to be issued your P1 licence.

i25 or older?

If you don't finish your Learner licence until you're 25 or older, you skip the P1 stage entirely and go straight to a P2 provisional licence — once you've held your Learner for 12 months and passed the practical test. See the P2 page for details.
