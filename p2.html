1Moving from P1 to P2

Tenure

Hold your P1 for 12 months

You need to hold your P1 (Red P) licence for a minimum of 12 months before you're eligible to upgrade.

Test

Hazard Perception Test

A mandatory online, computer-based assessment that tests your ability to spot and respond to traffic hazards. You must pass it to move from P1 to P2.

225 and older: a different path

If you graduate from your Learner licence at age 25 or older, you skip the P1 stage and jump straight to a P2 provisional licence — after holding your Learner licence for 12 months and passing your practical test. No Hazard Perception Test is needed for this path since you haven't held a P1.
