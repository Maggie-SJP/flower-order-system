Open Licence

The end of the graduated licensing journey — full, unrestricted driving privileges once your provisional tenure is complete.

20 yrs
Minimum age
1–2 yrs
Typical provisional tenure

1Age requirement

You can qualify for an Open licence at age 20, after completing the required tenure on your P1 or P2 provisional licences — typically 1 to 2 years, depending on the age you entered the provisional stage.

2How to apply

Apply online or in person at a Department of Transport and Main Roads Customer Service Centre once you're eligible.
