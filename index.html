Your keys to driving in Queensland

iHow the scheme works

Time-based

Minimum holding periods

Each stage has a minimum time you must hold it before moving on — 12 months as a Learner, 12 months on P1 — so experience builds up gradually rather than all at once.

Age-based

Faster path if you're 25+

Learners who don't get their provisional licence until age 25 or older skip the P1 stage entirely and move straight to P2, once they've held their Learner licence for 12 months and passed the practical test.

Test-based

A test at every gate

Road rules test to get your Learner licence, a practical driving test to reach P1 (or P2), and a Hazard Perception Test to move from P1 to P2.
