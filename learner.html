1Eligibility & getting started

Age

How old do you need to be?

At least 16 years old to sit the in-person written road rules test. You can be as young as 15 years and 11 months to enrol in PrepL, QLD's online interactive learning program, via the Enrol in PrepL portal.

Getting your licence

Prove identity & pass a test

You need to prove your identity and either:

  • Pass the written road rules test in person, or
  • Complete PrepL, the online interactive learning program (from age 15 years 11 months)

2The rules while you're a Learner

You must always drive with a supervisor who holds an open driver licence. If you're under 25, you must record a minimum of 100 hours of supervised driving (including 10 hours at night) in your logbook, and hold your Learner licence for at least 12 months before moving on.

3Keeping a logbook

Digital App

The official, free QLD Learner Logbook App tracks your trips and submits them online automatically. Your supervisor needs a unique email address to digitally verify and approve each trip.

Paper Logbook

A hardcopy logbook is also an option — it requires your supervisor's physical signature for every entry.

Pro tip: professional lessons with an accredited driver trainer count triple. You can log up to 10 hours with an instructor, which counts as 30 hours toward your 100-hour total.

4Supervisor requirements

Licence status

Must hold a current, valid open driver licence (not a learner or probationary licence) for at least one year for that vehicle class. It can't be expired, suspended or cancelled.

Blood alcohol limit

Must have a BAC under 0.05 — a zero BAC limit is strongly recommended.

Position

Must sit in the front passenger seat next to the Learner driver at all times while supervising.

Professional option

Can be an accredited, professional driver trainer or driving instructor. In QLD, anyone can act as your supervisor as long as they meet the rules.

Phone restrictions

If the Learner driver is under 25, the supervisor cannot use a mobile phone on speaker or hands-free while the Learner is driving, as it can distract them.

5Vehicle requirements

Display plates

Clearly visible "L" plates must be attached to the front and rear of the vehicle.

Roadworthiness

The vehicle must be legally registered, insured and roadworthy.
